Once the models could read documents, I stopped pasting lore into every chat. I kept a living file. Below are pages from it. Not the novel. The method.

Voice locks

  • Jack / Lancelot

    Internal voice is modern and crude. External speech is forced Arthurian and should sound worn, not lived.

  • Zahilla / Arthur

    Arthur's speech is formal. Zahilla's internal voice is mythic and vengeful. The split is the point.

  • Fang

    Sarcastic and soulful. She hates the name Excalibur. She is a character, not a glowing sword.

  • Time

    No hours, no minutes, no named seasons. If a model writes 'two hours later,' send it back.

Practice I actually enforce

  • Feed the prior chapter ending before you draft.
  • Cap a descriptor at two or three uses.
  • If you only asked for dialogue, do not let it rewrite the blocking.
  • When file reading fails, stop. Switch the tool.
  • Keep score. A 9/10 that cannot remember Camlann is inland is not a 9.
